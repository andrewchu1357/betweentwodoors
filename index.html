The Story

The Story

Between Two Doors is an original coming-of-age musical about Lily, a Chinese American high school student caught between her family's expectations for her future and her own passion for theater. As Lily tries to find where she belongs, she navigates family, friendship, ambition, and the fear of disappointing the people she loves — all while discovering what it means to choose a future for herself.

About The Creator

Isabella Zhu

Isabella Zhu is a 15-year-old writer and performer based in New York City. She began developing Between Two Doors approximately two years ago and is now working toward bringing the musical to the stage for its first production. She is in the National Childrens Chorus in NY and recently attended the National Youth Opera Academy in Santa Fe, where she performed in Odyssey.
